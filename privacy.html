KRIMS COMMAND

PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE DATE: SEPTEMBER 2026 • KRIMS CODE STUDIO PRIVACY COMPLIANCE
Core Commitment: Your privacy and data security are our top priorities. We do NOT sell user data, we do NOT train external commercial models on private chats, and we only collect the minimum telemetry strictly necessary to power leveling, server moderation, and virtual economy features.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Krims Command or sign in to our Web Dashboard via Discord OAuth2, we may process:

  • Discord Account Identifiers: Your Discord User ID, username, discriminator, and avatar hash (used to display your profile card and correlate level XP).
  • Server (Guild) Data: Server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and server settings configured by guild managers.
  • Activity Telemetry: Message counts, voice activity timestamps (duration spent in active channels), and reaction clicks for leveling and reaction-role distribution.
  • Virtual Economy Data: In-game KryloCoins balance, purchased virtual items, daily streak counts, and verification codes.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We never store private direct messages (DMs) or confidential chat history. Messages processed by the AutoMod v5 safety shield are analyzed in ephemeral memory for toxicity, slurs, and raid spam, and are immediately discarded.

3. How We Use and Protect Your Data

Data collected is strictly used to:

  • Calculate and render XP cards, voice level-ups, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Enforce configured AutoMod rules, anti-raid protection, and quarantine bad actors.
  • Synchronize server configurations made in the Web Dashboard.

All persistent data is stored in enterprise-grade Neon PostgreSQL cloud databases with SSL encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and AES-256 encryption at rest.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or third parties. Data is shared exclusively with our cloud infrastructure providers (Vercel, Neon Database) strictly to operate the service.

5. Data Retention & Right to Deletion

Users and server owners maintain full ownership of their data. If you remove Krims Command from your Discord server or wish to have all associated user telemetry permanently erased from our databases, you can request immediate purging by opening a ticket in our official support server or contacting our Data Privacy Team.
